# SUPER-STORE-SALES-ANALYSIS

1. Executive Summary

This project analyzes Super Store sales data using Power BI to evaluate sales performance, profitability, customer segments, and regional trends. The dashboard reports $1.57M in sales, $175K in profit, and 3.93 average shipment days.

2. Business Problem Statement

The business needs to understand sales trends, identify high-performing product categories, analyze customer preferences, and evaluate regional performance to support data-driven decisions.

3. Project Objectives
Analyze overall sales and profit.
Identify top-performing product categories.
Understand customer segments and payment preferences.
Evaluate state-level sales performance.
Analyze monthly sales and profit trends.
4. Dataset Overview

The dataset contains retail sales information, including product categories, customer segments, payment methods, shipping methods, states, regions, sales, profit, and order dates.

Tool: Power BI.

7. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Customer Segment: Consumer contributes 48% of sales.

Monthly Trends: Sales and profit fluctuate across 2019 and 2020.
8. Key Findings and Insights
Total sales are approximately $1.57M.
Office Supplies is the leading sales category.
Consumer customers contribute the largest sales share.
California has the highest sales among the displayed states.
Monthly sales and profit vary over time.
9. Business Recommendations
Analyze profit margins and discounts by product category.
Develop customer retention strategies for the Consumer segment.
Investigate growth opportunities in high-performing states.
Evaluate payment-related costs and returns.
Improve shipping efficiency and sales forecasting.
10. Conclusion

The project demonstrates how Power BI can transform retail sales data into meaningful business insights. The dashboard helps identify leading product categories, customer segments, and geographical markets. These findings support better decisions regarding sales performance, customer retention, and operational efficiency.
